# study-guides
A collection of open, community-driven study guides designed to support South African learners in mastering key concepts across various subjects. Inspired by the structure and accessibility of the Mind the Gap series(currently available in Grade 12), these guides provide concise explanations, worked examples, practice activities, revision notes, and exam preparation resources.

The project aims to create freely accessible, continuously improving educational content that can be adapted, reviewed, and expanded by educators, tutors, volunteers, and contributors. All material is maintained in Markdown to encourage collaboration and transparency through GitHub.

## Objectives
- Provide clear and accessible learning resources.
- Simplify complex concepts through examples and illustrations.
- Support classroom learning, self-study, and revision.
- Encourage community contributions and peer review.
- Maintain openly available educational content for all learners.
